# Outcome measurement and privacy

## Question
What outcome data may goal-x collect and retain to evaluate whether improvements help? Choose the boundary first: (A) local-only, opt-in aggregate counters with no goal text; (B) local-only per-goal measures derived from existing ledger data; (C) explicitly opt-in external telemetry; or (D) no new measurement. Also define opt-in, retention, and visibility expectations.

## Resolution
Measurement is local-only, opt-in, aggregate, and must not include goal text. User agreed to this privacy boundary. Consent UX, retention, and visibility remain open and must be settled before implementation.
