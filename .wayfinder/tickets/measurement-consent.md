# Measurement consent and retention

## Question
How should local aggregate measurement be enabled, exposed, and retained? Choose whether opt-in is per project or global; where users inspect/reset data; and a retention rule (for example, retain until manually cleared versus a bounded window).

## Resolution
Measurement is opt-in per project, inspectable and resettable in settings, with a bounded rolling retention period (90 days). It remains local-only and aggregate, with no goal text. User agreed.
